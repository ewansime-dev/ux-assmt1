## Concept Statement

"Silksong: Unthreaded", or just "Unthreaded", is a fan site dedicated to the popular video game, "Hollow Knight: Silksong". When visiting the site, users can explore different aspects of Pharloom from Silksong, through analyses of varying artistic elements that all focus on the smaller picture.

Unthreaded is a site for those interested in art and visual design, whether for studies, passion, or just curiosity. The site showcases an unexplored portion of Team Cherry's decisions that provides insight to visitors interested in not only the game, but also art and design. The in-depth exploration of artistic elements and theory aims to assist art or design students, and the underlying information of the game the site includes allows newcomers and casual fans to learn more.

What differs substantially from existing resources are a few key features of the website that have not been explored in other sites. First, Unthreaded includes a unique form of analysis: comparison between the first game and Silksong. Following the analysis of elements, the Comparison subpage explores how an element relates to "Hollow Knight", or how it differs. Second, Unthreaded focuses on "the smaller picture". Many online resources such as video essays and articles cover the bigger pictures of Silksong in a controlled, linear perspective. This site aims to inform visitors of the unexplored parts of the game, in a non-linear and interactive format.

The design of Unthreaded is interested in appealing to two distinct target users. The first user type consists of casual fans or newcomers. The former are those who have some experience in the game, enough to know a little about Pharloom, and the latter are those very new to the game, looking to explore new encounters deeper as they play the game. Both casual fans and newcomers typically have no knowledge of art or design principles, and would browse the website on a phone, in short sessions and likely for unusual purposes. The second user type consists of art or design students, those with adequate knowledge of Pharloom who visit the website looking to study the art of the game. These users would typically browse on a laptop or computer, in longer sessions to research one or two visual elements, comparing them to the first game and focusing on "the smaller picture".
