A hulking smith made of bells, with a small sleigh bell for a head and a great church bell for a body. She speaks in the royal "we" and, with her assistant Ballow, crafts tools for Hornet in exchange for Rosaries and Craftmetal.

Featured Character: Sprintmaster Swift

Sprintmaster Swift lives for speed. Found at the eastern frontier of the Far Fields, reached through Hunter's March once Hornet can Silk Soar, he challenges her to three races around his course, each track harder than the last. Win and he hands over a Rosary Necklace, a Beast Shard and finally a Mask Shard. Lose, and he cheerfully tells you to step to the challenge again.
