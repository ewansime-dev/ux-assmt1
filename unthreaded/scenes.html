Featured Scene: Coral Army

Deep in the Sands of Karak, ranks of coral-crusted warriors stand silent in the dark. They once served Crust King Khann in the Order of Karak, fighters bonded to the living coral and trained to master it. When the waters that sustained them were cut off, the Order withered away, leaving this eerie field of soldiers fading into the gloom.
